Whisper model weights
---------------------
The optional WhisperKit engine downloads the OpenAI Whisper large-v3 Core ML
conversion from Hugging Face (argmaxinc/whisperkit-coreml) at runtime. The
model is not part of this repository or the app bundle. OpenAI releases the
Whisper models under the MIT license.
